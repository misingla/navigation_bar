Read the two numbers separately. Section headers carry a tab-impression count (e.g. Deals & Quotes = 2,044,917) while each item carries a link-click count. They measure different events and must not be summed — “Create Estimate” alone (1,814,984) exceeds its own section header (1,042,154). Throughout, section share is computed from link clicks only.

Where the engagement actually is

The 15 most-clicked items, grouped by section, with each section’s share of the 10.38M link clicks. This is the single most important picture in the report: the menu’s visual weighting does not match its usage weighting.

    What this means in one paragraph

    The current menu spreads seventeen product categories and a long tail of rarely-touched tools across the most prominent real estate, while the areas people use most — renewals/subscriptions and the core create/view flows — are either buried or diluted by duplicated utility links. The redesign opportunity is not cosmetic: it is to re-weight the architecture to the evidence, promote Subscriptions & Renewals to a first-class area, consolidate the cross-cutting “my items” utilities, and prune the dead weight. The TO-BE Proposal does exactly this.
